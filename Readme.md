# Sign Language Translator (AI-Powered)

"Connecting people through technology — making sign language simple and accessible for everyone."

---

## About the Project

Communication is a basic human need, yet many people in the deaf and hard-of-hearing community face daily barriers when trying to express themselves or understand others.

This project was built to help solve that problem. Its goal is simple: to make communication easier by turning everyday text into clear American Sign Language (ASL) gestures. Using artificial intelligence, the app simplifies sentences and displays the matching hand signs in real time.

---

## Key Features

* AI Text Simplification: Uses a local AI model to simplify long or complex sentences into clear words ready for sign translation.
* Visual Sign Mapping: Automatically maps text to clear, accurate images of sign language gestures.
* Fast and Private: Runs 100% locally on your computer, meaning your data stays private, works offline, and requires no internet or paid services.
* Simple Interface: Designed on a single page so anyone can use it easily without distraction.

---

## How It Works

1. Enter Text: Type any sentence into the input box.
2. AI Processing: The app simplifies the text using a local AI model.
3. Gesture Display: The app fetches and displays the corresponding sign language images instantly.

Created to support inclusive communication and make sign language accessible to everyone.
